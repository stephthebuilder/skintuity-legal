Skintuity Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 17, 2026

Skintuity is a personal skin-health journal: you photograph your skin, answer short daily questions, and record the products you use, and the app shows you how your skin's appearance tracked against what you did. This policy explains what data Skintuity handles, where it goes, and the choices you have. We have tried to write it the way we would want to read it.

The short version. Your data exists to show you your own patterns. We don't run ads, we don't sell or share your data for marketing, and we don't track you across other apps or websites. Photos are analyzed by an AI provider only with your explicit, revocable permission. You can delete your account and everything in it, yourself, at any time.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address and a password (stored as a secure hash).
  • Skin photos you choose to take or upload, and the AI-generated appearance scores and descriptions attached to them.
  • Journal entries: the daily details you log, such as mood, itch, stress, sleep, activity, foods, suspected triggers, medications you record, and optional notes and reflections.
  • Products and sensitivities: products you add, ingredient lists read from label photos, and the personal allergen list you maintain.
  • Light-therapy sessions you log.
  • Subscription status (whether your account has an active Skintuity Pro subscription). Payment itself is handled by Apple — we never see your card details.
  • Service counters such as how many AI analyses your account ran recently, used for fair-use limits; these are automatically deleted within days.

Weather correlation (optional): if you turn it on, the app uses your device location in the moment to fetch local weather and pollen conditions and stores those conditions alongside your journal entry. Your location itself is never stored.

A request about photos

Please do not photograph intimate areas. Skintuity is designed for tracking visible skin — face, arms, hands, legs, torso and similar. Images of intimate or genital areas are not needed for anything the app does, and we ask that you never submit them. If skin in those areas concerns you, please see a clinician directly.

Where your data goes

Skintuity is built on a small number of service providers, each doing one job:

  • Supabase hosts our database, photo storage, and sign-in system. Your rows are isolated per account at the database level, and photos live in private storage reachable only through short-lived links.
  • OpenAI receives your skin photo when you run an analysis, and journal-derived text when you run Insights, and returns the descriptions you see. This happens only after you give explicit consent in the app, which you can withdraw at any time in Settings. Under our API terms with OpenAI, your content is not used to train their models.
  • RevenueCat processes subscription events from Apple so your Pro status survives reinstalls and new phones. It sees your account identifier and purchase history, not your health content.
  • Apple handles App Store distribution and all payment, as it does for every iOS app.

That's the complete list. No analytics brokers, no advertising networks, no data sales, and no cross-app or cross-site tracking of any kind.

What we never do

  • We never sell your data or share it for advertising.
  • We never use your photos or journal to train AI models.
  • We never show your data to other users — there is no feed, no sharing, no social layer.
  • We never diagnose. Skintuity describes appearance and patterns in your own logged data; it is a general-wellness journal, not a medical device, and it is no substitute for a clinician.

Children

Skintuity offers a mode for a parent or guardian to track a child's skin from the adult's own account. Accounts themselves are for adults; we do not knowingly let children open accounts or submit their own data. AI photo analysis in child-tracking mode is subject to the same explicit consent, given by the responsible adult.

Retention and deletion

  • Your content stays in your account for as long as you keep the account — the entire point of Skintuity is the longitudinal record.
  • Delete Account (in Settings) permanently removes your journal, photos, products, analyses, reflections, and the account itself. This is immediate and cannot be undone by us.
  • Records of subscription billing events are retained after account deletion, as required for financial bookkeeping and dispute handling. They contain no health content.
  • PDF reports you export are files on your device, under your control.

Security

All data moves over encrypted connections. Database access is enforced per-account at the database engine itself (row-level security), photos are stored privately with expiring access links, and AI requests are authenticated and rate-limited server-side. No system is perfectly secure, but the architecture is built so that even our own application code cannot read one user's data on behalf of another.

Your choices

  • Use the core journal without ever enabling AI photo analysis.
  • Withdraw AI consent at any time (Settings → Privacy) — analysis stops until you re-enable it.
  • Leave weather correlation off; location is then never used.
  • Export your record as a PDF whenever you want it.
  • Delete your account, in the app, without asking us.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will update this page and its effective date, and for significant changes we will tell you in the app before they take effect.
